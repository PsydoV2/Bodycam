The lens

  1. LOG_01

    Body-camera perspective. No crosshair floating outside your view — you see corners the way corners actually get read.

  2. LOG_02

    Photoreal lighting in Unreal Engine 5. Muzzle flash blinds. Shadows hide. Every surface reacts the way it should.

  3. LOG_03

    Grounded gunplay. Recoil is real, timing is real — discipline decides who walks away.

  4. LOG_04

    Audio with consequence. Reverb, occlusion, footsteps that give you away — or give the enemy away.

Modes in early access

  • Free-for-all. No teams, no mercy — last one standing wins the round.

  • Land a kill, advance a weapon. Full loadout progression, zero downtime.

  • Two squads, one objective: outlast the other side's kill count.

  • Plant, defuse, survive. One explosive, one chance to read the room.

  • Hold the zone. Every second inside it counts against the clock.

  • Overrun defense. Hold the line as the horde gets faster, louder, closer.

Early access note

Bodycam is under active development. What you play today is a strong foundation that will keep evolving — your feedback helps set priorities and tune the feel. Tell the team what lands, what needs work, and what you want to see next on the official Discord.
