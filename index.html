DISPATCH_08 // LOCKED & LOADED

Everything you know is getting rebuilt.

T-MINUS · FREE UPDATE --d --:--:--

Locked & Loaded is the biggest update Bodycam has shipped since launch — animations, weapon handling, audio, progression and a new map, rebuilt from the ground up. Free for everyone, live September 2, 2026.

  • Reloads, weapon handling and physical reactions now respond to stress, stance, ammo type and how close the fight is. Startle when something explodes near you.

  • A full rework of primaries, secondaries, melee, explosives and gadgets — hundreds of attachments, unlocked through RP as you play.

  • The pause menu is gone. Loadout changes happen on an in-hand tablet, and you're exposed the whole time you're looking at it.

  • Physics-based FPV drones and remote-detonated RC cars — new ways to see a room, or clear one, before you walk in.

  • Sound design rebuilt end to end — reverb, occlusion and footsteps get sharper and more consequential.

  • A new map, and the clearest look yet at where Bodycam's visuals are headed.

  • Performance passes and dozens of smaller fixes, tuned from feedback across the Locked & Loaded playtest.

"Absolute realism and immersion, like nothing seen or felt before." — Luca Dassier, Studio Director

The lens

  1. LOG_01

    Body-camera perspective. No crosshair floating outside your view — you see corners the way corners actually get read.

  2. LOG_02

    Photoreal lighting in Unreal Engine 5. Muzzle flash blinds. Shadows hide. Every surface reacts the way it should.

  3. LOG_03

    Grounded gunplay. Recoil is real, timing is real — discipline decides who walks away.

  4. LOG_04

    Audio with consequence. Reverb, occlusion, footsteps that give you away — or give the enemy away.
