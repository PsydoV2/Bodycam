Personal data

Last updated: 2026-08-31

This site is a static fan/portfolio project with no server backend. In short: it does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Newsletter & contact form

The newsletter field on this site is a visual demo only. It is not wired to any mailing service — nothing you type into it is sent, stored, or read by anyone. There is no real signup happening.

Embedded content

The Steam widget embedded on this site loads directly from store.steampowered.com. That embed is subject to Valve's own privacy policy and may set its own cookies — this site has no control over or access to that data.

Analytics

This site runs no analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party scripts beyond the Steam embed above.

Questions

For anything regarding the actual game Bodycam or Reissad Studio, see their official site. This page only covers this fan-made redesign project.
